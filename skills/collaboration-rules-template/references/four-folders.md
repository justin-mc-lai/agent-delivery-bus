# Four folders that can do work

## 1. Projects
Current commitments only.

Suggested fields:

- goal
- status
- decisions
- open tasks
- blockers
- next review date

## 2. Knowledge assets
Refined material ready for reuse.

Suggested types:

- playbooks
- postmortems
- templates
- decision records
- checklists

## 3. Inspiration inbox
Capture fast, process later.

Rules:

- no perfection
- no production side effects
- every item needs a later triage state: keep / refine / discard

## 4. Collaboration rules
The highest-leverage folder.

Include:

- identity and role of the AI
- output format
- quality bar
- forbidden actions
- when to ask vs when to act
- how delivery scheduling is allowed to happen
